# Contact

Keeprun is developed in the open on GitHub. That is the fastest way to reach the people who
build it, and answers there help everyone who has the same question.

## Questions and problems

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/{{REPO}}/issues/new). Include your Windows version,
the Keeprun version (`keeprun status` shows it), what you expected and what happened. The end of
`C:\ProgramData\Keeprun\agent.log` often shows the cause. Before you post logs, remove anything
private, such as connection strings or host names. Check [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting.html)
first for common problems.

## Feature requests

Vote on the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap.html) with a thumbs up on its GitHub issues, or open a new
issue that describes what you want to do and why. Real use cases decide what ships next.

## Security problems

Do not post details of a vulnerability in a public issue. Open an issue that asks for a private
channel, with no details, and a maintainer will contact you. The
[security model](/docs/security.html) describes how Keeprun is designed.

## Companies

For questions about using Keeprun in your organization, such as deployment at scale, update
mirrors or security reviews, open an issue or start with the
[deployment guide](/docs/deployment.html).
